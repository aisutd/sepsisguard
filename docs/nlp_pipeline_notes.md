I chose DistilBART because it preserved details more consistently in my earlier comparison. The pipeline ranks features by absolute score and turns the top three into readable source text without interpreting the scores as patient measurements or reporting their direction. Across four mock inputs, the final summaries retained the selected feature names, but the model mostly copied the source and sometimes omitted its caveat about diagnosis. Postprocessing removes extra spaces and keeps up to two complete sentences, while a feature-name check catches omissions but does not fully verify factual accuracy.

## Week 3: verification (Issue #26)

I switched the default generator from DistilBART to a fixed template that groups each patient's top SHAP features by whether they raised or lowered the predicted risk, and kept DistilBART as an optional backend whose output must pass verification or is replaced by the template. `verify_summary.py` finds every feature a summary mentions, including synonyms and codes like "pulse" or "MAP", and fails it if it names a feature outside the patient's top 5, gets a raised/lowered direction wrong, drops the caveat, or uses any wording the template does not, which also rejects diagnoses, outside reasons like "infection", and claims about measured values. My first version only rejected a list of bad phrases, and review showed it passed things like "shifted the risk upward" and "the patient may be septic", so it now fails closed. On the real SHAP export all 10 template and all 10 DistilBART summaries passed (the 10 rows cover only 2 patients, since they are different hours of the same stays), and on the 12 mock patients the template passed 12/12 while DistilBART passed 10/12, both failures being a caveat rewritten from "a diagnosis" to "diagnosis". The check cannot see what a summary leaves out: DistilBART silently dropped features in 2 of its 10 mock passes (p006 lost both risk-lowering features, p009 lost systolic blood pressure), so next I would fail summaries that drop a top-5 feature and, once the 12-hour export has feature values, check numbers too.
