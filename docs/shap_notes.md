Vitals such as respiration rate and heart rate make strong clinical sense since acute changes in breathing and hemodynamics are hallmark signs of physiological stress and potential sepsis. Furthermore, markers like lactate and body temperature directly reflect tissue hypoperfusion and fever responses, aligning closely with standard clinical screening protocols. While individual SHAP values vary based on specific patient baselines, their overall presence confirms that the model relies on vital physiological indicators monitored during critical care. Overall, these dominant features provide a reliable and clinically interpretable foundation for the sepsis risk scores.
